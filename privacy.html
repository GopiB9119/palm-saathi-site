Privacy policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Your farm notebook is stored on your device, not in a Palm Saathi account. The app does not sync it to a server. Some optional features do send information outside the app.

What stays in your notebook?

Your profile, plots, contacts you enter, jobs, field notes, sales, payments and their history, complaints, scheme checklists and service requests are local records. So are your language and saved guides. The Android app and web browser keep separate notebooks. There are no ads, analytics or crash-reporting services in the app.

Weather and location

Choose a town from the built-in list without giving location permission. Or allow Home to ask your device for your approximate location. With permission already granted, Home can check again automatically. The app takes individual fixes while in use; it does not track you in the background.

Home can refresh a forecast automatically. It sends the town’s coordinates or your device coordinates, rounded to two decimal places, directly to MET Norway over HTTPS. MET Norway logs the coordinates and your IP address. It also receives request headers identifying the app or browser. The Android app may include a public contact for the app operator, not your contact details or farm records. See MET Norway’s terms and log information.

The selected place and forecast are cached locally, outside notebook backups. Choose Forget place on the weather card to clear them and stop updates. You can also revoke location permission in device or browser settings.

Reading aloud and reminders

After you agree to read aloud, the device or browser voice engine receives public guide text in your chosen language. Depending on the voice, it may send the text to its provider over the internet. The app does not read private records aloud or record from your microphone. Reset speech permission in Support → Language & reading.

Optional Android reminders use the notification permission, local task identifiers and dates, and local alarms that can resume after a restart. Alerts do not contain private farm details. Turn them off in Support → Reminders.

When you open another app or site

A call opens your dialer with the number. For a service request, SMS receives the number and draft; if you confirm opening WhatsApp, WhatsApp receives both before you press Send. The draft can include names, village, plot, date, work details and price. You decide whether to call or send. Saving a request alone sends nothing.

Guide, price, scheme and support links open external sites. Those sites see normal browser requests, including your IP address, and apply their own policies. Copying a complaint or feedback puts it on your clipboard; it does not submit it.

Backups and deletion

A backup is an unencrypted copy of your notebook. The Android app disables automatic app backup. To make your own copy, use the clipboard, download a file in the browser or choose a file provider on Android. Other apps may read the clipboard; a cloud folder or synced clipboard can take a copy off the device. A restore replaces the notebook rather than merging it.

To erase this notebook, go to Support → Backup & data → Delete all local records and confirm. This removes the records, their history, local recovery copies and saved weather, and clears local reminders. It cannot erase exports, clipboard history, provider logs, messages or a notebook on another device or browser. Remove those copies separately where you control them.

This website and privacy questions

These information pages have no forms, cookies set by the pages, trackers or analytics. GitHub Pages logs visitors’ IP addresses for security. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.
