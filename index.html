From the field to the mill

  • Plan jobs and harvests. Keep field notes, expenses and contacts together.
  • Record deliveries and compare payments with the amounts due.
  • Read field guides in English or Telugu. Check a local forecast when online.

The notebook works offline. Recording a request does not book a crew or apply for a subsidy. Palm Saathi is not a government service.
